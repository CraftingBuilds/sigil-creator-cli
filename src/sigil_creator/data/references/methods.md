# Sigil Creation Methods Reference

## 1. Austin Osman Spare / Chaos Magic Letter Reduction

Origin: Austin Osman Spare, *The Book of Pleasure* (1913); systematized in chaos magic (Peter J. Carroll *Liber Null*, Ray Sherwin).

Steps:
1. Formulate Statement of Intent (SoI): Present tense, positive, specific, as if already true. Prefer "I am..." or "It is my will that..." Avoid negatives and future tense.
2. Optional: Remove all vowels (A E I O U, sometimes Y).
3. Remove all repeated letters, keeping only the first occurrence of each unique letter. Case-insensitive; work in uppercase.
4. Arrange remaining letters into a single abstract glyph: overlap, rotate, share lines, stylize until original letters are unrecognizable. Aim for balance, compactness, aesthetic unity.
5. Optionally enclose in a circle or other geometric frame.

Variations:
- Keep vowels for more complex designs.
- Mantra form: reduce to pronounceable syllables for chanting during charge.
- Word method: use a single keyword instead of full sentence.

## 2. Planetary Kamea (Magic Square) Method

Origin: Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa, *Three Books of Occult Philosophy* (1533), Book II. Roots in earlier Arabic and Chinese magic squares.

Planetary associations and sizes:
- Saturn: 3x3, constant 15, total 45 — restriction, protection, discipline, endings, time, boundaries.
- Jupiter: 4x4, constant 34, total 136 — expansion, wealth, success, authority, growth.
- Mars: 5x5, constant 65, total 325 — energy, courage, conflict, protection, action.
- Sun: 6x6, constant 111, total 666 — vitality, success, recognition, illumination, leadership.
- Venus: 7x7, constant 175, total 1225 — love, relationships, beauty, harmony, pleasure.
- Mercury: 8x8, constant 260, total 2080 — communication, intellect, commerce, travel, skill.
- Moon: 9x9, constant 369, total 3321 — intuition, emotions, cycles, dreams, the subconscious.

Letter-to-number (simple Western, A=1 ... Z=26, then reduce by digital root or Aiq Beker / Nine Chambers if needed to fit square):
A=1 B=2 C=3 D=4 E=5 F=6 G=7 H=8 I=9
J=10=1 K=11=2 L=12=3 M=13=4 N=14=5 O=15=6 P=16=7 Q=17=8 R=18=9
S=19=1 T=20=2 U=21=3 V=22=4 W=23=5 X=24=6 Y=25=7 Z=26=8

For squares smaller than needed numbers, reduce via digit sum or Aiq Beker (groups of 9).

Construction:
1. Choose planet matching intent.
2. Reduce SoI letters to unique or sequential numbers as preferred (common: sequential unique letters after reduction).
3. Locate cells on the kamea in order of the numbers.
4. Draw continuous path connecting centers of cells. Start with small open circle, end with short crossbar or filled circle (Agrippa convention).
5. Stylize the resulting polyline into a refined seal if desired. May add outer circle.

Exact squares are public domain mathematical objects; agent should reconstruct or recall standard Agrippa arrangements when needed (or use script).

## 3. Rose Cross Method (Golden Dawn)

Origin: Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, late 19th century. Based on the Rose Cross Lamen with 22 petals corresponding to Hebrew alphabet (or adapted English).

Steps:
1. Obtain or visualize the Rose Cross diagram: equal-armed cross with multi-petaled rose at center; petals arranged in three rings bearing the 22 Hebrew letters (or English phonetic equivalents).
2. Convert intent or name into sequence of letters (Hebrew preferred for traditional; English common in modern use).
3. Starting from the first letter's petal, draw a continuous line connecting successive letters' positions on the rose.
4. Begin the line with a small circle, end with a bar.
5. The resulting path is the sigil. May be extracted and refined standalone.

Note: Full accurate petal layout requires reference image or standard GD diagram. Agent can describe or approximate connections; for precision use image generation guided by description.

## 4. Traditional Grimoire Style

Pre-existing seals (e.g., Goetic spirits from Lesser Key of Solomon, planetary seals from Agrippa, Solomonic pentacles) are not "created" from personal intent but reproduced or adapted. For personal work:
- Combine planetary seal + spirit seal + divine names + geometric forms (circles, hexagrams, pentagrams).
- Or create new in similar aesthetic: angular lines, circles, Hebrew/Latin names, crosses, using kamea path as core.

Do not invent or claim authenticity for specific spirit seals without accurate source. Prefer directing user to public-domain grimoires or describing construction principles.

## Charging and Activation (Common Across Traditions)

- Gnosis: Enter altered state (meditation, orgasm, exhaustion, sensory overload, hyperventilation, dancing, mantra, etc.) while focusing on the sigil.
- Visualization: Gaze at sigil until it seems to move or glow; project intent into it.
- Physical: Draw in appropriate material/color/metal; burn, bury, carry, or destroy after charging.
- Forget: Deliberately banish the original intent from conscious mind so the unconscious can work.
- Timing: Planetary day/hour for kamea-based; lunar phase as appropriate (waxing for increase, waning for decrease).
- Release: After charging, destroy the physical sigil or put it out of sight and stop thinking about the desire.

Psychological model (Spare/chaos): Sigil encodes desire for the subconscious; conscious mind is bypassed via abstraction + gnosis + forgetting.
